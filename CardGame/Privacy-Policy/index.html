1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Game, we or our service providers may collect or process:

  • Guest account information: Pouch & Peril currently uses Unity Authentication to create an anonymous Player ID. You do not need to provide an email address or password to begin playing.
  • Profile information: Your chosen display name, selected in-game profile icon, account creation date, and unlocked profile icons.
  • Game and progression data: Owned cards and artifacts, deck and artifact loadouts, virtual currencies and tickets, card-pack activity, mailbox rewards, tutorial completion, challenge attempts, shop state, ad-reward limits, purchase entitlements, and similar progress information.
  • Statistics and leaderboard information: Scores, ranks, highest level, lifetime and single-round statistics, challenge results, display name, and profile icon. Information submitted to a leaderboard is visible to other players.
  • Purchase information: Product ID, transaction ID, purchase token or receipt, price, currency, purchase status, and fulfillment status. Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Advertising and device information: If ads are requested or displayed, Unity LevelPlay and participating ad networks may process advertising identifiers, device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, device and app information, ad views, interactions, reward events, and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Technical information: Device type, operating system, app version, language, connection data, diagnostics, errors, and service logs needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Game.
  • Communications: Information you voluntarily include when contacting us by email or joining and using the separately operated Discord community.

The Game does not ask for your contacts, photographs, microphone recordings, health information, or precise GPS location. A profile icon is selected from artwork unlocked in the Game and is not a user-uploaded photograph.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide guest authentication and maintain your account;
  • Save progress, collections, decks, artifacts, balances, purchases, and settings;
  • Operate leaderboards, challenges, drafts, rewards, mail, shops, and tutorials;
  • Display and verify rewarded advertisements and enforce daily limits;
  • Process, validate, fulfill, restore, and support purchases;
  • Detect cheating, manipulated scores, fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  • Diagnose problems and improve reliability, balance, features, and performance;
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests; and
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Advertising

Pouch & Peril offers rewarded-ad opportunities. Some purchases may allow eligible ad rewards or refreshes to be claimed without watching an ad while the purchased no-ads benefit is active. Daily limits and other eligibility rules may still apply.

Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay and may be provided by mediated advertising networks. Those providers may use information to deliver ads, measure performance, limit repeated ads, provide rewards, prevent fraud, and comply with law. Depending on your location, the Game or an advertising provider may request consent or offer privacy choices. You can also manage advertising-ID and personalization controls in your device settings.

4. Purchases and Virtual Items

Google Play processes real-money payments. Unity IAP and Unity Economy are used to validate receipts and deliver eligible in-game rewards. Purchase records may be stored to prevent duplicate fulfillment, restore non-consumable entitlements, resolve interrupted deliveries, provide support, and prevent fraud.

Virtual currencies, cards, artifacts, tickets, shards, bundles, daily-delivery benefits, and no-ads entitlements are associated with your Game account. Google may separately collect and retain payment and transaction information under its own policies.

5. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information for money. We may disclose or allow information to be processed by:

  • Unity Technologies: Unity Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Economy, Leaderboards, IAP, LevelPlay advertising, security, and related game services;
  • Google: Google Play distribution, billing, purchase validation, device services, and related platform functions;
  • Advertising partners: Networks participating through Unity LevelPlay;
  • Technical providers: Hosting, security, diagnostics, and support providers used to operate the Game;
  • Legal and safety recipients: Authorities, advisers, or other parties when reasonably necessary to comply with law, investigate fraud, protect users, or enforce our rights; and
  • Business-transfer recipients: A successor or relevant party in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, sale, or transfer of the Game or business.

Leaderboard display names, icons, scores, and ranks are shared with other players as part of the Game. Personalized advertising may be treated as a "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising under some privacy laws even when no money is exchanged for the information.

Third parties process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Relevant policies include:

Retention periods may differ by data type and provider.

Some records may remain after an account-deletion request when retention is required for legal compliance, payment records, refunds, security, fraud prevention, or the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims. Where practical, retained information will be limited or de-identified.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect Game information. No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

TriBush and its service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Privacy protections in those locations may differ from those where you live.

Pouch & Peril is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information was collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. Users who are not old enough to consent under applicable law should use the Game only with permission from a parent or legal guardian.
