Delete Your Account & Data

You may request deletion of your Pouch & Peril guest account and associated Game data at any time.

Before requesting deletion

Deletion is permanent. Your collection, currencies, purchases delivered to the Game, progression, statistics, loadouts, mail, profile, and other account data cannot be restored after deletion is completed.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Open Pouch & Peril.
  2. Open your profile, then open the Account page.
  3. Copy the Player ID displayed there.
  4. Email tribushonline@gmail.com with the subject Pouch & Peril Account Deletion Request.
  5. Include your Player ID, current display name, and the statement: “I confirm that I want this Pouch & Peril account and its associated data permanently deleted.”

Because accounts currently use anonymous authentication, the Player ID is necessary to locate the correct account. Send the request before uninstalling or clearing the Game if possible.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Guest authentication account and Player ID, where technically available;
  • Profile name and selected or unlocked profile icons;
  • Card and artifact collection, decks, and loadouts;
  • Game currencies, tickets, shards, entitlements, and store state;
  • Progression, lifetime statistics, challenge state, tutorial state, and mailbox data;
  • Leaderboard information associated with the account, where supported by the provider.

Information That May Be Retained

Limited transaction, refund, fraud-prevention, security, support, and legal-compliance records may be retained when required or permitted by law. Google Play separately controls payment and order records held in your Google account. Where practical, retained records will be restricted or de-identified.

Timing

We aim to acknowledge deletion requests within 7 days and complete verified requests within 30 days. We may contact you if additional information is reasonably necessary to identify the account or prevent unauthorized deletion.

Deleting Only Certain Data

If you want to request access, correction, or deletion of particular information without deleting the entire account, email tribushonline@gmail.com and describe your request. Include your Player ID so we can locate the relevant guest account.
