    BlockSurvival and Project Insight live here.

    TriBush focuses on two releases right now: BlockSurvival, a fast one-finger dodger newly launched on Google Play in September 2025, and Project Insight, a Unity editor tool that maps every asset, dependency, and unused file in your project. Dive into the guide, see what shipped, and track what’s next.

    Play BlockSurvival Project Insight on Asset Store

    BlockSurvival

    Slide your finger side to side to dodge a storm of falling blocks. Chase your best score in Classic mode or duel a friend in Battle mode.

    One-finger control Arcade pacing Battle mode

    Project Insight

    Scans folders, scenes, and assets to produce readable .txt reports of every GameObject, component, property, and unused asset across your Unity project.

    Hierarchy export Unused asset detection Configurable reports

    BlockSurvival launches on Google Play
