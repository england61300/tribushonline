    BlockSurvival

    Your guide to the arcade dodger.

    BlockSurvival is a clean, fast-paced arcade game where you slide your finger to dodge falling blocks. Outlast the storm as the difficulty ramps in real time, chase your best score, or face an opponent in Battle Mode.

    Download on Google Play Roadmap

    Quick facts

    • Release: September 2025 on Google Play.
    • Modes: Classic score chase & Battle Mode.
    • Controls: One-finger slide to dodge.

    Next update targets

    How to play

    Master the basics to push past the first wave and set new personal bests.

    Start here

    Controls & goals

    Keep one finger on the screen and slide left or right to steer. Survive as long as possible while dodging every block—contact ends the run.

    Focus on staying centered so you can react to either lane.

    Difficulty ramps

    The fall speed and spawn rate increase the longer you stay alive. Watch for subtle pacing cues that warn when the storm is about to intensify.

    Quick, short swipes are safer than wide, last-second moves.

    Battle Mode

    Challenge a friend or rival. Both players dodge identical patterns and whoever survives longer claims the win.

    Stay calm—the match swings fast once the speed spikes.

    Survival tips

    A few fundamentals to help you outlast the block storm.

    Stay in motion

    • Glide continuously to avoid freezing up when clusters spawn.
    • Use the corners sparingly—blocks can stack there quickly.
    • Practice relaxing your grip to keep swipes precise.

    Reset between rounds

    • Take a breath before the next attempt to keep timing sharp.
    • Review which patterns tripped you up and adjust positioning.
    • Play a few short warm-up runs to get a feel for the speed curve.
