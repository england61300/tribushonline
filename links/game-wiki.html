    Biomes & resources

    Get quick intel on what to expect, what to harvest, and which enemies call each biome home.

    Ember Barrens

    A volcanic wasteland riddled with magma vents and obsidian pillars. Ideal for farming blaze shards and thermal cores.

    • Heat management gear is mandatory.
    • Rare drop: Ember Wisp familiar (5% spawn).
    • Corruption surge every fourth night.

    Frostspire Wilds

    Frozen cliffs connected by brittle ice bridges. Packed with crystalized sap and huskwood for late-game alchemy.

    • Watch for shatter drakes swooping at dusk.
    • Thermal insulation buffs stack up to three times.
    • Hidden vault unlocks experimental blueprints.

    Enemy intel

    Telemetry-backed stats to help you counter every threat.

    Rift Stalkers

    Fast melee units that phase through single-layer walls. Counter with layered barricades or shock traps.

    • Average DPS: 36/sec
    • Spawns on threat level 3+
    • Drop chance: Corrupted sinew (42%)

    Howler Brood

    Ranged units that debuff stamina with sonic blasts. Insight data shows they cluster near light sources—use that to funnel them.

    • Average DPS: 18/sec
    • Spawns on threat level 2+
    • Drop chance: Resonant shards (27%)

    Titan Husk

    Mini-boss with armor phases tied to elemental weaknesses. Requires stagger damage to expose the core.

    • Health pool: 12,500 HP
    • Recommended gear: Shock lance or explosive runes.
    • Drop chance: Titan heart (guaranteed)
