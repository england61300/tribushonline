    From networks to narrative systems

    A quick tour of the experiences that shape how I build today.

    Foundations

    I grew up in a tight-knit community, balancing sports with early forays into technology. Curiosity led me from psychology to plant science, and eventually into the United States Marine Corps as a network administrator.

    Takeaway: adaptability and resilience fuel my problem solving.

    Transition to creation

    After service, I pursued work that blended structure with creativity. Insurance kept the lights on, but game development and tooling offered the spark I couldn’t ignore. BlockSurvival started as a weekend prototype and grew into a live release.

    Takeaway: structure is a launchpad for imagination.

    BlockSurvival

    A fast, one-finger arcade dodger launched on Google Play in September 2025. I’m currently building skins, daily rewards, buffs, and an economy system for the first post-launch update.

    Project Insight

    A Unity toolkit that scans folders, scenes, and assets to export readable reports. It captures every GameObject, serialized property, and unused asset so teams can audit their projects quickly.

    Outside of dev

    Things that keep me grounded.

    Community & mentorship

    I volunteer with aspiring developers, helping them break down intimidating problems and see their ideas through to a prototype.

    Curiosity-driven learning

    From networking certifications to creative writing workshops, I’m constantly learning to bridge technical and narrative craft.

    Well-being

    When I’m not at the keyboard, you’ll find me exploring hiking trails or experimenting with plant propagation—reminders that growth takes patience.
