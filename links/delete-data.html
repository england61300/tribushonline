    Player privacy

    Request data deletion

    To delete your BlockSurvival data, email tribushonline@gmail.com with the details below. Email is the only channel used for verification and confirmation.

    Start an email Read the policy

    What to include

    Copy this template into your message so I can verify ownership and fulfill the request quickly.

    Email template

    • Subject: “Data deletion request – BlockSurvival”.
    • Game name: BlockSurvival.
    • Player ID: (found in the in-game settings menu).
    • Email used in-game or for purchases.
    • Confirmation statement: “Yes, delete my data.”

    Send the email from the address associated with your account whenever possible.

    Processing timeline

    • Requests are acknowledged within 7 days.
    • Deletion completes within 30 days unless follow-up info is required.
    • You’ll receive a confirmation email once data has been removed.
