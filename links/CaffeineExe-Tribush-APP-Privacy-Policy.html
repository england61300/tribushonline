Caffeine.exe Rewards Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Caffeine.exe Rewards is a mobile rewards application developed and operated by TriBush LLC ("TriBush," "we," "us," or "our"). The app allows users to earn reward points, claim daily rewards, watch rewarded advertisements, use referral codes, redeem points for discount codes, and access the Caffeine.exe online coffee store.

Caffeine.exe is the online coffee store where reward discount codes may be used. Store browsing, checkout, payments, shipping, and order-related activity are handled through the Caffeine.exe website and Shopify-powered store services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you download, access, or use the Caffeine.exe Rewards mobile app.

1. Information We Collect

The Caffeine.exe Rewards app may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Anonymous app account information: The app uses anonymous authentication services to create and manage an app user account without requiring you to provide a name, email address, or password inside the app.
  • Reward activity: We store your points balance, daily reward claim status, referral code, referral usage status, redeemed coupon history, discount code records, and related app reward activity.
  • Advertising and device information: If you choose to watch rewarded ads, Unity Ads and related service providers may collect or process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app activity, approximate location derived from network information, ad interaction data, and diagnostic information.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: We or our service providers may process information such as device type, operating system, app version, crash data, performance data, and logs to maintain, secure, and improve the app.
  • Store interaction information: If you open Caffeine.exe from the app, use a discount code, add products to cart, or complete checkout, information related to browsing, cart, checkout, payment, shipping, and orders may be collected and processed by Caffeine.exe, Shopify, payment processors, and other store service providers.

We do not require users to create a named account inside the app. The app does not directly request payment card information, shipping addresses, phone contacts, photos, camera access, microphone access, or precise GPS location. Payment and shipping information may be collected only if you choose to shop through the Caffeine.exe store.

2. How We Use Information

We use information collected or processed through the app for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the Caffeine.exe Rewards app;
  • To save your points balance, coupons, referral code, and reward history;
  • To allow daily claims, rewarded ad rewards, referrals, and discount redemptions;
  • To create and manage discount codes that can be used on the Caffeine.exe store;
  • To prevent abuse, duplicate referrals, fraudulent reward activity, or misuse of coupons;
  • To display rewarded advertisements when you choose to watch them;
  • To troubleshoot errors, improve app performance, and maintain app security;
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms, policies, and rights.

3. Rewarded Ads

The app may show rewarded advertisements through Unity Ads or related advertising services. Rewarded ads are optional. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, you may receive app reward points after the ad is completed.

Advertising providers may collect or process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad views, ad interactions, app activity, and diagnostic information. These providers may use this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and comply with their own legal obligations.

You may be able to limit personalized advertising through your device settings. On Android, you can review your advertising ID and ad personalization settings in your device's privacy or ads settings.

4. Referrals and Reward Codes

The app includes a referral feature. Each app user may receive a referral code. If another user enters your referral code, the app may record that referral activity and award points according to the current reward rules.

Referral data is used to operate the referral system, prevent self-referrals, prevent repeated referral claims, detect abuse, and maintain accurate reward balances.

When you redeem points, the app may generate a discount code for use on the Caffeine.exe store. Redeemed coupon records may include the coupon code, reward type, discount amount, point cost, claim date, and expiration date.

5. Shopify and the Caffeine.exe Store

The Caffeine.exe store is powered by Shopify. If you leave the app and visit the Caffeine.exe website, use a discount code, add items to cart, or complete checkout, your activity on the store may be processed by Caffeine.exe, Shopify, payment processors, shipping providers, analytics providers, and other store service providers.

Store-related information may include contact details, billing and shipping information, payment-related information, order details, cart activity, device information, usage information, and communications with the store.

Store purchases, payment processing, shipping, returns, and order support are governed by the Caffeine.exe store policies and Shopify-related privacy practices, not only by this app policy.

6. Service Providers and Third Parties

We may share or allow processing of information with service providers that help us operate the app and connected store services, including:

  • Unity / Unity Gaming Services for authentication, cloud save, ads, diagnostics, and app services;
  • Unity Ads or related advertising partners for rewarded advertisements;
  • Shopify for store, cart, checkout, discount, and ecommerce services;
  • Payment processors if you make a purchase through the Caffeine.exe store;
  • Hosting, analytics, security, and technical service providers that help operate and protect the app and store;
  • Legal, compliance, or fraud-prevention parties when required to protect users, comply with law, or enforce our rights.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable service terms.

7. Information We Do Not Directly Collect in the App

The Caffeine.exe Rewards app does not directly collect the following through the app interface:

  • Payment card numbers;
  • Shipping addresses;
  • Phone contacts;
  • Photos or videos;
  • Microphone recordings;
  • Precise GPS location;
  • Health, biometric, or sensitive personal information.

Some of this information, such as payment or shipping information, may be collected only if you choose to visit the Caffeine.exe store and complete a purchase.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The mobile app itself may use software development kits, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, analytics tools, cloud services, and similar technologies to operate app features, rewarded ads, diagnostics, security, and fraud prevention.

The Caffeine.exe website and Shopify-powered store may use cookies, pixels, analytics, advertising tools, and similar technologies when you visit the store through the app or through a browser.

9. Data Retention

We retain app reward information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain reward balances, prevent fraud or abuse, troubleshoot issues, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our policies.

Coupon records, point balances, referral status, and related app data may be retained while your anonymous app account remains active or while retention is reasonably needed for app operations, security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or business records.

Store-related information, such as order and payment records, may be retained by Caffeine.exe, Shopify, payment processors, and other store service providers according to store policies, legal requirements, and provider retention practices.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

The Caffeine.exe Rewards app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

The app is intended for a general audience and is not designed as a child-directed app. Users who are not old enough to use the app or store under applicable law should not use the app without appropriate permission from a parent or legal guardian.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection to certain processing.
